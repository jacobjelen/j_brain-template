# John's second brain (j_brain)
John keeps a second brain at `~/j_brain`. It holds notes on him, his people, projects, clients, skills, knowledge, taste, lifestyle and health, written for AI agents.
- **Don't read it up front.** Use it when a task would benefit from personal context: who someone is, a project's state, his preferences or plans. Also use it when John mentions something you don't know.
- **When you use it:** search first with `python3 ~/j_brain/system/scripts/recall.py <names or keywords>`. It returns each matching note's summary, TL;DR and matched lines. Open a full note only if that isn't enough. Obey `~/j_brain/system/rules/lessons.md`. Skill: `brain-recall`.
- **Capturing:** when John says "remember…" or shares a lasting fact, save it to `~/j_brain/inbox/` (skill `brain-remember`). Don't edit anything else there unless he asks. Read `~/j_brain/AGENTS.md` before writing.
